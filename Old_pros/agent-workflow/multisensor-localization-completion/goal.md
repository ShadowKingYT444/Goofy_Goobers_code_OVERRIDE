# Goal

Make tournament localization work from robot-mounted sensors using a known exact start pose, drivetrain encoders, the rear-center lateral odometer, IMU, four-sensor LiDAR wall observations, and onboard AI Vision observations of the nine static tagged field props. LiDAR may be blocked. The laptop webcam may be used only as temporary debugging ground truth and must not contribute to the operational pose. Movement is limited to roughly 3 feet total and rotation to less than 720 degrees.
