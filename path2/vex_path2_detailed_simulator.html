Path 2 phase checklist

  1. Reverse into the toggle, then pull back out to the start lane.
  2. Immediately score the preload on blue goal 2 at (24, -48).
  3. Back off and rotate to the first stack at (24, -24), slow down on approach, then intake it.
  4. Raise the lift to level 1 while returning to goal 2 and score there.
  5. Repeat for the second stack at (48, -48), but raise to level 2 on the way back.
  6. Back off again, rotate so the rear/claw is oriented toward the matchloader, lower the lift while traveling, and finish at the blue matchloading station.
